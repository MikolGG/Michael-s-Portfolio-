About

I'm a product designer working on products for Nigerian users. On SENDIT I was the only designer, so I handled everything from research to final UI.

The biggest lesson from that project: my first assumption was wrong. I thought delivery cost was the main problem, but interviews showed that trust, tracking and communication mattered more. I now treat research as the way to find out what I'm wrong about.
